How it works

Standard Catan is a fixed hexagon. Pangaea builds free-form continents instead — but free-form shapes make far more coastline than the official board, and coastline quietly wrecks resource balance.

The core problem

Every intersection — where a settlement can go — touches 1, 2 or 3 tiles. A 3-tile spot collects three resources' worth of rolls. Reshape the same tiles into a spikier outline and the tile count doesn't change, but 3-spots turn into 1- and 2-spots.

A
land tiles
S
land-to-land tile edges — how many pairs of tiles touch
v1 v2 v3
intersections touching 1, 2, 3 tiles
Smax
the most S possible for A tiles — a perfectly round blob
L
enclosed lakes (bodies of water, however many hexes)

Euler's formula locks these together:

v3 = S − A + 1 − L
v2 = 3A − S − 3 + 3L
v1 = 3A − S + 3 − 3L      (the generator fills lakes, so L = 0)

So v3 depends only on S. The maximum is Smax = ⌊3A − √(12A − 3)⌋; every edge short of it destroys one 3-spot and creates one 1-spot and one 2-spot.

What the generator does

  1. Grow + compactify — grow a continent, then hill-climb tile positions until S reaches the coastline floor.
  2. Place ports — space port slots evenly along the outer coast.
  3. Anneal — thousands of random swaps of tiles, number tokens and ports, keeping better boards always and worse ones sometimes, less often as the run "cools". That tolerance early on is what stops it getting stuck.
  4. Score — simulate a 1→N→1 snake draft and report how fair it was.

Each candidate is penalised for a 6 or 8 touching another 6/8, identical numbers touching, same-terrain clumps, resource pips out of line with tile counts, 2:1 ports far from (or on top of) their resource, and — most of all — a simulated draft where the best and worst seats end up far apart.

Game tracker

Tap Tracker during the game. Everyone joins with their profile and a color nobody else has; the game is the map on screen, so New map starts a fresh game for everyone — you keep your seat and color, and season wins are kept. It tracks victory points (13 wins), each player's honor coin (flip your own), knights on the main island and off island, cities, karma, and the robber — Collect takes the robber's value off karma.

WALKER gets a letter each time a 7 is rolled with the barbarians — tap +. As the word fills, the letters shake harder and winter sets in on every phone: pixel snow and frost. + on the full word starts it over and the snow melts away.

On another phone or computer, pick your profile on the welcome screen: if you're in the game you're straight back at the tracker, and the music picks up where you were.

Music

Tap ♪ to open the music bar: your YouTube or YouTube Music playlist (public or unlisted) with previous / play / next, shuffle and the song list. It stays docked under the map and the tracker — on the right when the phone is sideways — so the phone you cast to the TV shows the board and plays the music. Paste the link once and every phone has it; change it with the 🔗 button. On a phone, start the first song by tapping the video; after that the buttons work. Closing the bar pauses the music.

On a computer, the music and the tracker share a column on the left of the map — drag the edges to resize the column, the split between them, and the settings on the right.

Have YouTube Premium? Under 🔗, Play in the YouTube Music app hands the playlist to the app, which keeps playing in the background with no ads — the bar closes and the map gets the whole screen, and screen mirroring still sends the music to the TV.

Install it as an app

Pangaea installs straight from the browser — no app store — and then opens full screen, without the browser bars. iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen (keep Open as Web App on). Android: tap the phone icon at the top, or Install app in the browser menu. The app keeps a copy of itself, so it opens and makes maps with no signal, and it updates itself whenever you're online.

On iPhone the installed app has its own storage, separate from Safari: the first time you open it, pick your profile on the welcome screen and you're straight back in your seat, music and all.
